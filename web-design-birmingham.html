I build websites for women in business, charities and community groups in Birmingham and the West Midlands: fixed price, yours to own, and built so nobody writes you off before you’ve said a word.

Who is this for?

  • Female founders whose website is the last thing still looking “temporary”.
  • Charities and community groups who need donors, volunteers and bums on seats.
  • Women in the trades who want to be booked for their skill, not questioned about it.
  • Anyone with that je ne sais quoi who refuses to look like everyone else.

How does it work if we’re not in the same room?

Really well, honestly. Within two days of your enquiry you get a plan with real dates. Then you get your own client page: upload your logo and photos, see what’s done and what’s next, and never have to type “any update?”.

What will my website actually do for me?

  • Say what you do in the first line, in the words your customers use.
  • Put your proof where the doubt is: reviews, results, real photos.
  • Get found in Birmingham searches, on Google and in AI answers.
  • Work beautifully on a phone, because that’s where people find you.
Not sure where to start? Send me your current site, or your Instagram if that’s all you’ve got, and I’ll tell you honestly what I’d do first.

Questions people in Birmingham ask

Do you work with businesses in Birmingham?

Yes, Birmingham and across the West Midlands. Most of the work happens on screen anyway: you get your own client page where you can watch your site being built, so you’re never chasing me for an update.

How much does a website cost in Birmingham?

From about £300 for a simple one-pager to £4,500+ for bookings, payments and custom tools. It’s a fixed quote agreed before anything starts, with no monthly rent.

Do you work with charities and community groups?

Yes. Charity and community websites are a big part of what I do, with honest advice about what your budget will stretch to.

What if I already have a website?

Start with the Doubt Test. I’ll tell you what’s worth keeping, and if your site is fine I’ll say so.
