The deal

What you can expect from me. What I expect from you.

Great websites aren’t done to you. They’re built with you. Here’s exactly what each of us brings.

From me

  • I work to the plan and the dates. You get real deadlines in two working days, and I hit them.
  • I put my all into it. Your site gets the same 69 checks and the same care as if it were my own.
  • Straight talk. I’ll tell you what works and what doesn’t, even when it’s not what you want to hear.
  • You’re never left guessing. Your project page shows where we are, every day.
  • A fixed price. No padding, no surprise bills.
  • I’m in your corner. Your success is the whole point.

From you

  • You know what you want, or you’re ready to dig in and work it out with me.
  • You do what you say you’ll do. Words, photos, feedback and sign-offs, by the dates we agree.
  • You work with my deadlines, because a launch date only works if we both keep to it.
  • You’re reliable. You reply, you turn up to calls, and you tell me early if something changes.
  • You’re serious about getting your website working to plan, not just “getting one up”.
  • You’re honest with me, so I can be honest with you.

Expertise

Trained not to miss a single part.

I’m a full-stack developer trained at Code Institute, and I’ve studied how the best web agencies run their projects. The Dragon Fire Process turns that into a checklist I follow on every single build.

  • The same 69 checks, every time. Quality doesn’t depend on whether I’m having a good day.
  • Best practice built in. Accessibility, speed on a cheap phone, security and clean code, checked before launch.
  • Found, not just finished. Every site goes through Dragonfire, my own SEO tool, for Google and AI search.
  • One person who understands the whole build. Words, design, front end and back end, so nothing gets lost in a hand-off.
