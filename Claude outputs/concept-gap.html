The concept gap

The space between what a stranger (or an AI) can guess about your business, and what it actually is.

Left sideAI can only work from patterns it's already seen. Ask it about your business and it fills in the blank with what's normal for your trade — not what's actually true about you.
The gapThat's the distance between a generic-sounding site and one that sounds like you. Nothing closes it on its own.
Right sideThe stuff only you know — why customers stick around, what you're proud of, what's different about how you work. It only gets onto the site if someone actually asks you.
