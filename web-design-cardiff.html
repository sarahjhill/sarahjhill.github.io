Who is this for?

You, if any of this sounds familiar:

  • You’ve started something and your website still looks like a placeholder.
  • You run a charity, CIC or community group and need people to donate, volunteer or turn up.

What makes a Cardiff website different?

Cardiff is a city that looks after its own. People here buy from people they trust, and they notice when you’ve bothered. So:

  • Bilingual when it matters. Cymraeg first or English first, your call, done properly.
  • Local proof up front. Where you work, who you’ve helped, real photos of you.
  • Found on the map. Set up for Google so people nearby can find you when they search.

Questions people in Cardiff ask

How much does a website cost in Cardiff?

Anywhere from about £300 for a simple one-pager to £4,500+ for a site with bookings, payments and custom tools. Whatever you pick, you get a fixed quote before anything starts, and no monthly rent.

Do you build bilingual Welsh and English websites?

Yes. Cardiff Community Meals is Cymraeg first, with English one tap away, because it’s Cardiff’s. If your community speaks Welsh, your website should too.

Do you work with charities and community groups?

Yes, and gladly. Charity and community work is the first world on my homepage for a reason. I’ll be straight about what your budget can do, and what it can’t.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the brief. Within two days of your enquiry you get a plan with real dates, and you can watch the build happen live in your own client page.

I’m not techy. Is that a problem?

None at all. Plain English, one small step at a time, and only when it’s your turn.
